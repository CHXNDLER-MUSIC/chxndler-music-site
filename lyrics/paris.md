# PARIS

[Verse 1]
Neon and rain on the Avenue Ciel
Perfume and secrets we promised to tell
Your lipstick on coffee, a map on my skin
We drew a future and colored it in

[Pre-Chorus]
Lanterns are floating above Sacré-Cœur
We keep our balance on rooftops and blur

[Chorus]
Kiss me in Paris, even if it hurts
Love tastes like poison, but I drink it first
Under the rivers of gold in your eyes
I’d fall again in a heartbeat tonight

[Verse 2]
Taxi cab halos and flicker-light scars
We wrote our names in a city of stars
Pages and pages we’ll never return
But I keep the ashes to feel how they burn

[Bridge]
If morning finds us strangers at last
I’ll keep the photograph safe in the past

[Final Chorus]
Kiss me in Paris, even if it hurts
Love tastes like poison, but I drink it first
Under the rivers of gold in your eyes
I’d fall again in a heartbeat tonight
